About Pharoah Group

Our History

Pharoah Group is a luxury and supercar dealership based at 3 Winnie Mandela Drive, Bryanston, Sandton. Founded by Scott Pharoah and officially incorporated in South Africa in October 2006, the dealership has grown from a single idea into a multi-division business built around vehicle sales, customisation, and lifestyle experiences.

The idea behind Pharoah Group was that buying a car should be more than a transaction — it should be a moment. What began as a passion for machines grew into a culture built on luxury, celebration, and connection, expressed today through in-house divisions like 6IXPCK Customs, Skär Wheels, Espresso, FOAM Car Wash, and Black Velvet Rentals.

Our Founder

Scott Pharoah

Scott Pharoah founded the dealership on the belief that luxury vehicle ownership should feel like joining a community, not just closing a sale — a philosophy that still shapes every part of the business today.

Our Mission

To deliver more than a vehicle sale — combining access to the world's most sought-after luxury and performance brands with a culture-driven, white-glove ownership experience.

Our Vision

To be Southern Africa's most trusted name in luxury vehicle ownership, known as much for the experience it creates as for the cars it sells.

Who We Serve

We proudly serve high-net-worth individuals aged 35–65, business executives, entrepreneurs and private collectors, located primarily in Gauteng and the Western Cape, as well as a growing segment of international buyers looking to import vehicles into South Africa.
